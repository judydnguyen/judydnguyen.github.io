## Howdy!

I'm Judy | CS Ph.D. student @ Vanderbilt | [SoICT](https://soict.hust.edu.vn/en/) alum | [VinUni](https://vinuni.edu.vn/) research alumni | Vietnamese blood in me.
Advised by [Prof. Taylor T. Johnson](http://www.taylortjohnson.com/) & [Prof. Kevin Leach](https://kjl.name/).

**About myself.** I’ve been slowly figuring out who I want to be and what kind of life I want to live, knowing I only get to do this once. It matters to me that the things I build can grow in impact and depth over time. As I move forward, the pieces of my childhood dreams that once felt divergent are now starting to intertwine again. A lot of that comes from my obssession with sci-fi movies about AI and endlessly automated, replicated “everything,” which left me with a question: *How can we ensure AI remains controllable, reliable, and aligned with human values?*.

**About myself (more technically).** My research explores *security, privacy, and ethical vulnerabilities in AI systems*, and is now expanding toward the *safety and alignment of large language models and agents*. I strongly believe that AI should serve humanity by making people feel both **safer** and **happier** in their everyday lives.

**Beyond research.** I’m passionate about photography, books, and music, and I almost never say no to a new adventure. You can call me Judy, or, if you’re curious, you can try my real name Dung, pronounced */zʊŋ/*.
